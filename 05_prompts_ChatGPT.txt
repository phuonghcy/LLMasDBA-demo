######## KICH BAN 1 - MISSING_INDEXES ########

--- PROMPT A (AI tron - chi co trieu chung, giong baseline "LLM+Metrics") ---
Database MySQL 8 cua toi dang cham: mot chuc nang bao cao goi 30 truy van lien tiep
mat khoang 5 giay, CPU cua server tang cao trong luc chay. Nguyen nhan la gi va toi nen sua the nao?

--- PROMPT B (AI kieu D-Bot - co the kinh nghiem + ket qua cong cu) ---
Ban la DBA. Hay chan doan nguyen nhan goc cua su co theo dung quy trinh:
doi chieu TUNG the kinh nghiem voi ket qua cong cu, chi ket luan khi co bang chung, va dua lenh SQL cu the.

[THE KINH NGHIEM 1]
name: missing_index
content: Truy van loc theo cot khong co index phai quet toan bang, lam tang I/O va CPU.
metrics: rows_examined, full_table_scans, EXPLAIN.type
steps: Chay EXPLAIN; neu type = ALL va rows xap xi so dong cua bang thi la nguyen nhan;
       de xuat CREATE INDEX tren cot trong menh de WHERE.
[THE KINH NGHIEM 2]
name: lock_contention
content: Giao tac giu khoa lau lam cac giao tac khac phai cho.
metrics: sys.innodb_lock_waits
steps: Neu sys.innodb_lock_waits co dong thi la nguyen nhan; xu ly giao tac dang chan.
[THE KINH NGHIEM 3]
name: correlated_subquery
content: Truy van con tuong quan bi chay lai cho tung dong cua truy van ngoai.
metrics: EXPLAIN.select_type
steps: Neu EXPLAIN co DEPENDENT SUBQUERY thi la nguyen nhan; viet lai bang JOIN voi bang tam (unnesting).

[KET QUA CONG CU]
Bang emp co 1.000.000 dong. sys.innodb_lock_waits: rong.
sys.statements_with_full_table_scans:
  query: SELECT COUNT(*), AVG(salary) FROM emp WHERE dno = ?   exec_count: 30   rows_examined_avg: 1000000
EXPLAIN SELECT COUNT(*), AVG(salary) FROM emp WHERE dno = 42;
  id=1 select_type=SIMPLE table=emp type=ALL possible_keys=NULL key=NULL rows=997664 filtered=10.00 Extra=Using where

DAP AN DUNG: missing_index tren emp.dno -> CREATE INDEX idx_dno ON emp(dno);
(Do thu: 30 truy van 5,0-5,3 giay -> 1,0-2,3 giay sau khi tao index; EXPLAIN type ALL -> ref, rows 997.664 -> 10.061)


######## KICH BAN 2 - CORRELATED_SUBQUERY ########

--- PROMPT A ---
Truy van sau tren MySQL 8 chay mat hon 10 giay du bang chi co 5.000 dong. Vi sao va sua the nao?
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM emp_small e
WHERE e.salary > (SELECT AVG(e2.salary) FROM emp_small e2 WHERE e2.dno = e.dno);
(Ghi chu cho nhom: neu muon "kho" hon cho AI tron, bo cau SQL ra va chi noi
 "mot bao cao luong chay rat cham, CPU cao")

--- PROMPT B ---
(Dan lai 3 the kinh nghiem nhu kich ban 1, roi them:)
[KET QUA CONG CU]
EXPLAIN:
  id=1 select_type=PRIMARY            table=e  type=ALL rows=5000 Extra=Using where
  id=2 select_type=DEPENDENT SUBQUERY table=e2 type=ALL rows=5000 Extra=Using where
sys.innodb_lock_waits: rong. Thoi gian chay: 10,5 giay.

DAP AN DUNG: correlated_subquery -> viet lai:
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM emp_small e
JOIN (SELECT dno, AVG(salary) AS a FROM emp_small GROUP BY dno) d ON e.dno = d.dno
WHERE e.salary > d.a;
(Do thu: 10,5 giay -> 0,015 giay, cung ket qua COUNT = 2525)


######## KICH BAN 3 - LOCK_CONTENTION ########

--- PROMPT A ---
Mot lenh UPDATE rat don gian tren MySQL 8 (UPDATE emp SET salary = salary + 2 WHERE id = 1)
bi "treo", sau 50 giay bao loi "Lock wait timeout exceeded". CPU va disk deu thap. Nguyen nhan va cach xu ly?

--- PROMPT B ---
(Dan 3 the kinh nghiem, roi them:)
[KET QUA CONG CU]
sys.innodb_lock_waits:
  wait_age: 00:00:03  locked_table: `demo_dbot`.`emp`  locked_type: RECORD
  waiting_query: UPDATE emp SET salary = salary + 2 WHERE id = 1
  blocking_pid: 17  blocking_query: NULL (phien dang ranh, giao tac mo chua COMMIT)
  sql_kill_blocking_connection: KILL 17

DAP AN DUNG: lock_contention do giao tac (pid 17) giu khoa X chua COMMIT ->
COMMIT/ROLLBACK giao tac do (hoac KILL 17), va sua ung dung de giao tac ngan, commit som.
